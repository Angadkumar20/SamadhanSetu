## 📌 Problem Context

Across regions like Jharkhand, citizens, local bodies, and government agencies identify thousands of hyper-local challenges daily—spanning agriculture, healthcare, water management, sanitation, rural livelihoods, and urban infrastructure. However, these real-world problems often remain unresolved due to the absence of a unified, structured workflow.

Simultaneously, **Higher Education Institutions (HEIs)** possess significant research and student talent, while **Industries & Startups** bring funding, mentorship, and deployment capabilities. **SamadhanSetu** bridges these fragmented silos to drive demand-driven, technology-enabled innovation aligned with **NEP 2020**.

---

## 🚀 Solution Overview

**SamadhanSetu** is a centralized, AI-powered digital platform that crowdsources societal challenges, automates domain categorization and university assignment, and fosters cross-sector collaboration among **Citizens, Universities, Industry Partners, and Government Agencies**.
